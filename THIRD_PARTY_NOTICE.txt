Third-party libraries in this directory
=======================================

  Prawn 2.5.0      https://github.com/prawnpdf/prawn      ./LICENSE.txt
  pdf-core 0.10.0  https://github.com/prawnpdf/pdf-core   ./pdf/LICENSE.txt
  ttfunk 1.8.0     https://github.com/prawnpdf/ttfunk     ./ttfunk/LICENSE.txt

The original distributions are available at the addresses above and at
https://rubygems.org.
The source code as modified for SHK 3skeng is available at
https://github.com/soundglider/3skeng-prawn

Each library may be used under its LICENSE conditions, GPLv2 or GPLv3. SHK 3skeng
uses them under the LICENSE conditions, not under the GPL. The COPYING and GPL
files are upstream's and are kept unchanged.

Modifications made by SHK Engineering and Consulting GmbH & Co KG
-----------------------------------------------------------------

- All modules are placed in the Tsk3 namespace (Tsk3::Prawn, Tsk3::PDF, Tsk3::TTFunk).
- Internal load statements use Sketchup.require.
- BASEDIR and DATADIR point to this directory layout.
- Upstream sample fonts are removed. The Adobe AFM files and MustRead.html are kept.
- Comments and one error message contain small text corrections.

Installed 3skeng packages may contain these Ruby sources in encrypted form (.rbe).

prawn/fonts/*.afm are Adobe Core 14 font metrics under the terms in
prawn/fonts/MustRead.html.
